Product hunting

I research products against real demand and real competition — not just what's trending — and run the margin math before anything gets recommended. You get a shortlist with reasoning behind each pick, not just a list of names.

  • Competitor density
  • Supplier cost & margin
  • Platform fit

Store audit

A full pass through your existing store — listings, pricing, compliance, and SEO — with a plain-language report on what's actually costing you sales, ranked by what to fix first.

Product listing

  • Main & secondary images

  • Platform image specs

Store management

The recurring work that keeps a store healthy after launch — order handling, inventory sync, customer messages, and a standing report on what's working.
