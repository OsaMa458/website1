About

I work on one thing: stores that actually sell.

No agency, no team, no upsells you don't need — just the work that moves a store from "listed" to "selling."

I help Walmart Seller Center and TikTok Shop sellers who don't have the time — or don't want to guess — at product research, listing quality, or day-to-day store admin.

Every project starts with an honest look at what's actually holding the store back, then gets fixed in the order that moves sales fastest. No padded scopes, no work you didn't ask for.

  1. 01 — Audit & discovery I review your store or product idea and tell you exactly what's working, what isn't, and what it'll take to fix.
  2. 02 — Sourcing & setup Products researched and vetted, store or listings structured correctly from the start.
  3. 03 — Listings, SEO & graphics Every listing written, tagged, and designed to rank and convert on its specific platform.
  4. 04 — Ongoing management Orders, inventory and store health handled on a set cadence, with a standing report on results.
  • Who I work with Small and mid-size sellers launching or already running a Walmart Seller Center or TikTok Shop store.
  • Platforms Walmart Seller Center, TikTok Shop.
  • How projects start A short call or message about where your store is at, then a scoped plan before any work begins.
  • What's not included General web development, unrelated marketing services, or anything outside the store itself — kept off-scope on purpose.
